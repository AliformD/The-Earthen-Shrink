# The Earthen Shrink

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon for Shamans (Elemental, Enhancement and Restoration).
It reads your stats, gear and talents and shows stat priority, weapon buff advice,
damage per mana pool, and maximum DPS, all inside the game.

Status: first playable version (0.2.0). The addon is in [`TheEarthenShrink/`](TheEarthenShrink/README.md): weapon buff math, spell damage per mana for every rank, and inspecting other players.
Research notes: [docs/research.md](docs/research.md). Test outside the game with `lua5.1 tests/fake_game.lua TheEarthenShrink`.
